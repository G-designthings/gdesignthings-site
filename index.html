Services

The Logo

A clean, professional logo built around your brand's core idea. Hand-crafted logo design, no templates.

  • Short brief, then hand sketches in several directions
  • Chosen direction built digital, refined with you
  • Primary logo in colour, black and white
  • All source files and full ownership

Brand Identity System

A full visual identity. Logo suite, color system, typography and brand guidelines built to last.

  • Everything in The Logo
  • Logo system: primary, secondary, icon and lockups
  • Colour palette, typography and a brand book with usage guidelines
  • Brand assets: patterns, templates and mockups, ready for print, web and social
