Why sketches come first

A logo is easy to redraw on paper and expensive to redraw on screen. That's why we lock the direction by hand before anything goes digital. You see the idea early, we adjust it in minutes, and the digital phase only has to make a decision you've already approved look great.

Good to know

Questions

How long does a project take?

First pencil sketches arrive 4 to 7 days after the deposit. A logo usually takes 2 to 3 weeks from start to final files, a full brand identity 4 to 6 weeks. The pace depends mostly on how fast we decide together.

How does payment work?

Two payments, each with an invoice. 50% books your spot and covers the sketch phase. The remaining 50% is due when you choose your direction, before digital development begins. Final files follow your approval, with nothing more to pay. You can pay by credit card through a PayPal invoice, or by PayPal, Wise, bank transfer or PayID.

What if none of the sketches feel right?

That is what the sketch phase is for. From your brief I draw several directions, not one. We go through them together, what works and what is missing, and I come back with new solutions. I am here to solve the problem and find the right one with you. If we still cannot find it, you stop there. The deposit covers the sketches and you owe nothing more.

What do I get at the end?

Every file you will ever need: AI, EPS, SVG, PDF and PNG, in colour, black and white, with a colour codes sheet. For a brand identity, also the colour palette, typography, guidelines and brand assets such as patterns, templates and mockups. Full ownership of the final logo once paid in full.

Do you use templates or AI to make logos?

No. Every mark starts as a pencil sketch drawn for your business. Nothing is generated and nothing is reused from another client.

Can we talk before I decide?

Yes. Write a few lines about your business to ask@gdesignthings.com. I come back with questions before prices. If a short call helps, I will send a link.

Do you work with clients outside Bosnia?

Yes, worldwide. Most of my clients are in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia and across Europe. Everything runs online, in English, with invoices and payment options that work in any country.
