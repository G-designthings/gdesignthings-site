Privacy
Policy.

Short and plain. What I collect, why, and what you can ask me to do with it.

01

Who I am

g.designthings is a brand identity studio run by Goran Jugovic, registered in Bosnia and Herzegovina as Agency G.DESIGNTHINGS s.p. Goran Jugovic, Majke Jugovica 8, 78430 Prnjavor (JIB 4512373400004). I am the data controller for this website.

02

What I collect

When you write to me through the contact form, email, WhatsApp or Instagram: your name, email, phone number if you share it, and what you tell me about your business and project.

When you become a client: the details needed for the offer, the invoice and the work itself. Payments go through Wise, PayPal or bank transfer. I never see or store your card number.

When you visit the site: anonymous usage data, see Analytics below.

03

Why I collect it

To answer your message, write an offer, run the project, send invoices and keep the records the law asks me to keep. Nothing else. I do not sell your data and I do not send newsletters you did not ask for.

Legal basis: your request before a contract, the contract itself once we start, my legal duty to keep invoices, and my legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used.

04

Analytics and ads

The site uses Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel. They tell me how many people visit, which pages they read, and whether an ad brought them here. The data is aggregated and does not name you. Google and Meta may set cookies for this. You can block them in your browser or with an ad blocker, and the site still works.

05

Who else sees it

Only the services that run this studio, each under its own privacy terms: Cloudflare (hosting), Web3Forms (contact form delivery), Google Workspace (email and files), HubSpot (lead notes), Make (automation), Wise and PayPal (payments), Meta and Google (analytics). Some of them are outside the EU. I share no more than each one needs.

06

How long I keep it

Messages from people who never became clients: up to 2 years, then deleted. Client files, offers and invoices: as long as the bookkeeping law requires, usually 10 years. Analytics data: as set by Google and Meta, with anonymisation on.

07

Your rights

You can ask me at any time what I hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it. Write to ask@gdesignthings.com and I answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
